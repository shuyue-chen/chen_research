Ongoing Translation: The Records of Shu (Chapter 3), in Chang Qu’s Records of Huayang

A concise statement explaining what this project is.

This project aims to translate the third chapter of this gazetteer – the Records of Shu (Shu zhi 蜀 志). Shu is the alternative name of Chengdu and/or the region of Sichuan. The third chapter contains roughly 7,900 characters and provides an encyclopedic knowledge of this region from remote times to the fourth century. It is not only an early regional history of Shu, but also a collection of descriptions of local tales, natural resources, geographical features, and customs. Additionally, it describes the administration of each prefecture and county in this region.
