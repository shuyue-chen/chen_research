Translation: Records of Shu (Chapter 3), in Records of Huayang

This project aims to translate the third chapter, Records of Shu in Records of Huayang, into English. Shu is the alternative name of Chengdu and/or the region of Sichuan and nearby land. The third chapter contains roughly 7,900 characters and provides an encyclopedic knowledge of this region from remote times to the fourth century. It is not only an early regional history of Shu, but also a collection of descriptions of local tales, natural resources, geographical features, and customs. Additionally, it describes the administration of each prefecture and county in this region.

This is an ongoing translation project supported by Institution of East Asian Studies at UC Berkeley.
