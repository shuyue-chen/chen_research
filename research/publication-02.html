The Power of Folk Music: City Branding, Musical Imaginaries, and Tourism-Induced Placemaking in Yulin, Chengdu (China)

September 2023

This paper is a case study of Yulin, a neighborhood in Chengdu, China. It describes how contemporary folk music attracts tourists to a particular neighborhood and inspires city branding and investments, which dramatically transforms the built environment and cultural idenity of the neighborhood.
