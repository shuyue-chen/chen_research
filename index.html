Shuyue Chen

Shuyue Chen is an Architecture PhD student at UC Berkeley with degree emphasis in history, theory, and society. Her research focuses on vernacular architecture, everyday urbanism, space in literature, tourism, and urban imaginary. Her dissertation explores the urbanization of Chengdu and the interpretation of Chinese modernity in the 1990s.
