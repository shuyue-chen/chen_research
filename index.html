Institutional Profile


Shuyue Chen is a PhD candidate in architecture history, theory & theory, at UC Berkeley. Her research focuses on vernacular architecture, everyday urbanism, literary space, tourism, and urban imaginary. Her dissertation explores the urbanization of Chengdu, the interpretation of Chinese modernity, and the creation of modernized citizens in the 1990s and 2000s.
